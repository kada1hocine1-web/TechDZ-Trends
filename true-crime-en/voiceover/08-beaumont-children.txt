THE BEAUMONT CHILDREN (1966, ADELAIDE, AUSTRALIA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Adelaide, Australia. January 26th, 1966. Australia Day.

Three siblings, Jane, Arnna and Grant Beaumont, aged nine, seven and four, take the bus to Glenelg Beach.

They are expected home by midday. They never return.

Witnesses later report seeing the children playing with a tall, thin man with fair hair.

At a local shop, the children pay for their food with a one-pound note, more money than their mother had given them.

The disappearance triggers one of the largest searches in Australian history.

Over the decades, sites are excavated, and new leads emerge. None bring them home.

The Beaumont children have never been found.

Their disappearance changed Australia forever, and the case remains open to this day.
