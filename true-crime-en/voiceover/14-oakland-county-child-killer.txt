THE OAKLAND COUNTY CHILD KILLER (1976, MICHIGAN, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Oakland County, Michigan. 1976.

Over thirteen months, four children are abducted and killed.

Mark Stebbins. Jill Robinson. Kristine Mihelich. Timothy King.

Their bodies are found days later, left in public places, carefully laid out.

Fear grips the suburbs of Detroit. Parents stop letting their children go out alone.

A task force launches what was then one of the largest murder investigations in American history.

Thousands of tips are examined. Over the years, DNA testing rules out several suspects.

No one is ever charged.

The case remains open, and investigators still hope for a breakthrough.
