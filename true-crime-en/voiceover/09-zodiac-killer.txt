THE ZODIAC KILLER (1968, CALIFORNIA, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Northern California. December 1968.

A killer begins a series of attacks. Over the next ten months, five people are killed and two survive.

Then he starts writing to the newspapers.

He calls himself the Zodiac, and signs his letters with a crosshair symbol.

He sends coded messages, ciphers, and demands they be printed on front pages.

One cipher is cracked within days by a schoolteacher and his wife.

Another, the 340 cipher, resists for 51 years. Amateur codebreakers finally solve it in 2020.

In his letters, the Zodiac claims as many as 37 victims.

Thousands of suspects are investigated. No one is ever charged.

The Zodiac Killer was never caught.
