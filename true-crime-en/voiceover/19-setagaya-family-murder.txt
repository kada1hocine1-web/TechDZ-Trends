THE SETAGAYA FAMILY MURDER (2000, TOKYO, JAPAN)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Setagaya, Tokyo. The night of December 30th, 2000.

The Miyazawa family, a father, a mother, and their two young children, are at home.

An intruder enters through a bathroom window and kills all four.

Then, instead of fleeing, the killer stays in the house for hours.

He uses the family computer, eats food from the fridge, and even treats his own wounds.

He leaves behind fingerprints, his blood, and clothes. Evidence that should solve any case.

DNA suggests that his mother's family line may trace back to southern Europe.

Yet his fingerprints and DNA match no one in Japan's databases.

More than two decades later, Tokyo police are still asking the public for help.

The killer has never been identified.
