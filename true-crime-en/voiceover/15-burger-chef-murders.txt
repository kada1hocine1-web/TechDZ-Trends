THE BURGER CHEF MURDERS (1978, INDIANA, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Speedway, Indiana. November 17th, 1978.

Four young employees close up a Burger Chef restaurant for the night.

Jayne Friedt, Ruth Ellen Shelton, Daniel Davis and Mark Flemmonds. The oldest is twenty.

Later that night, the restaurant is found unlocked and empty. The day's earnings are gone.

At first, police think the staff took the money and left. The scene is cleaned before it is properly examined.

Two days later, the four are found dead in a wooded area in a neighboring county.

Investigators follow thousands of leads over the decades.

No one has ever been charged.

The Burger Chef murders remain one of Indiana's most haunting unsolved cases.
