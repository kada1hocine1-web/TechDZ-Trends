THE MONSTER OF FLORENCE (1968, FLORENCE, ITALY)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Florence, Italy. Between 1968 and 1985, the hills around the city become a hunting ground.

Eight couples are murdered, sixteen victims in all, attacked at night in parked cars in secluded spots.

Ballistics link the killings to the same .22 caliber Beretta pistol.

The press names the killer 'Il Mostro di Firenze', the Monster of Florence.

The investigation becomes one of the longest and most controversial in Italian history.

In 1994, a farmer named Pietro Pacciani is convicted. His conviction is overturned on appeal. He dies in 1998 before a retrial.

Two of his acquaintances are later convicted for some of the murders.

But many investigators and journalists doubt that the whole truth was ever uncovered.

The gun was never found.

Decades later, the true identity of the Monster of Florence is still debated.
