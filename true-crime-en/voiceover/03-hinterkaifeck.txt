THE HINTERKAIFECK MURDERS (1922, BAVARIA, GERMANY)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Bavaria, Germany. 1922. An isolated farmstead called Hinterkaifeck.

Days before the end of March, farmer Andreas Gruber notices footprints in the snow, leading from the forest to the farm. None lead back.

He hears footsteps in the attic. A set of house keys goes missing. He mentions it to neighbors, but never goes to the police.

A new maid arrives on March 31st. The previous one had quit, saying the house was haunted.

That night, all six people on the farm are killed, including two children.

The bodies are discovered days later, on April 4th.

The most chilling detail: someone appears to have stayed on the farm after the murders, feeding the animals and eating food from the kitchen.

Police question over a hundred suspects over the decades. No one is ever charged.

The farmhouse is demolished the following year.

A century later, the Hinterkaifeck murders are still unsolved.
