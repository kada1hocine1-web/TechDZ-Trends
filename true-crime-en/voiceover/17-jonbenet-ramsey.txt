JONBENÉT RAMSEY (1996, COLORADO, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Boulder, Colorado. December 26th, 1996.

Early in the morning, Patsy Ramsey calls the police. Her six-year-old daughter, JonBenét, is missing.

On the stairs, she has found a long, handwritten ransom note demanding 118,000 dollars.

That afternoon, JonBenét is found dead in the basement of the family home.

The investigation is criticized from the start. The house was not fully secured, and evidence may have been compromised.

For years, suspicion and media attention fall on the family.

In 2006, a man falsely confessed to the murder. DNA ruled him out.

In 2008, new DNA testing leads the district attorney to formally clear the Ramsey family, and apologize.

Boulder police say the investigation is still active.

Nearly three decades later, JonBenét's killer has never been identified.
