THE BLACK DAHLIA (1947, CALIFORNIA, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Los Angeles, January 15th, 1947.

In a vacant lot in the Leimert Park neighborhood, a woman walking with her child discovers a body.

The victim is Elizabeth Short, a 22-year-old aspiring actress.

The newspapers give her a name that will make her famous: the Black Dahlia.

The crime is brutal, and the press coverage is relentless.

Days later, a package arrives at the Los Angeles Examiner, containing her birth certificate, photos, and address book.

Dozens of people come forward to confess. None of the confessions hold up.

Detectives investigate hundreds of suspects.

But no one is ever charged.

More than 75 years later, the Black Dahlia murder remains one of Los Angeles's most famous unsolved cases.
