THE ALPHABET MURDERS (1971, NEW YORK, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Rochester, New York. Between 1971 and 1973.

Three young girls, aged ten and eleven, are abducted and murdered.

Their names: Carmen Colón. Wanda Walkowicz. Michelle Maenza.

Each girl's first and last names begin with the same letter.

And each was found in a town starting with that same letter: Churchville, Webster, and Macedon.

The press calls them the Alphabet Murders, or the Double Initial Murders.

Hundreds of people are questioned. Several suspects are investigated and later ruled out.

No one has ever been charged.

Half a century later, the families are still waiting for answers.
