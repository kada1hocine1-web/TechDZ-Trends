THE VILLISCA AXE MURDERS (1912, IOWA, USA)
Read in order, with a pause of about one second between lines. Tone: calm, serious true-crime narrator.

Villisca, Iowa. June 1912. A quiet small town in the American Midwest.

On the night of June 9th, the Moore family returns home from a church program, with two young guests: the Stillinger sisters.

The next morning, the house is silent. Neighbors find all eight people inside murdered in their beds.

The weapon is an axe belonging to the father, Josiah Moore.

Strange details haunt investigators: every mirror and window had been covered with cloth.

Crowds trample through the house before the scene is secured, destroying evidence.

Several suspects emerge. A traveling preacher, Reverend George Kelly, is tried twice.

The first jury deadlocks. The second acquits him.

The case is never solved.

Today, the Villisca Axe Murder House still stands, and the killer's identity remains unknown.
